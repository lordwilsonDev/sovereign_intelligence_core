# Operational Proving Ground — Silent Mission Protocol

## Purpose
Conduct a 7‑day, unattended “Silent Mission” with daily chaos injection and a culminating public demonstration. Prove the sovereign organism’s resilience, self‑healing, and autonomous operation.

## Phase 1 — Initiation
- Activate the Chaos Scheduler STAR job to fire a random scenario every 24 hours.
- Silence human intervention: no manual restarts, no code changes, no config edits for 7 days.
- Ensure the Observer’s Log and SNH notifications are the only monitoring channels.

## Phase 2 — Daily Monitoring
- Once daily, review the Observer’s Log for anomalies.
- Check the ORS (Operational Readiness Score) trend.
- Do not intervene unless a critical safety violation occurs (SAC veto, Echo block, etc.).

## Phase 3 — Final Report
- On day 7, trigger a research mission: “the operational resilience of the MSB v3 sovereign organism.”
- The report must use the Axiom Library as its primary evidence source.
- Publish the report as a permanent artifact.

## Phase 4 — First Contact Demo
- On day 8, run the First Contact protocol from a fresh user’s perspective.
- Record the session and store it in the Axiom Library as `public‑demo`.

## Phase 5 — ORS Validation
- The Cognitive Mirage Auditor examines the ORS trend and chaos injection results.
- If ORS claims high readiness but chaos results show unhealed failures, flag ORS as a mirage.
- Publish the final ORS validation report.

## Meta‑Rules
- All phases are logged to the Observer’s Log and the audit chain.
- The SAC and Echo harnesses remain active throughout.
- If the organism cannot self‑heal within the chaos recovery window, the mission is paused and the human is notified.
